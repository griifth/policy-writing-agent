# Extraction Schema

Use this schema for one book, one author, or one selected chapter set. Omit unsupported fields rather than inventing them.

## 0. Record Header

- `title`
- `author`
- `year`
- `source form`
- `coverage level`
- `materials used`
- `working language`

## 1. Evidence Ledger

Track where each major claim comes from.

- `chapter or section anchors`
- `file or excerpt anchors`
- `evidence density`: sparse / moderate / strong
- `known gaps`

## 2. Book Thesis

- `one-sentence core claim`
- `central policy question`
- `intended audience`
- `declared problem to solve`

## 3. Explicit Analytical Framework

Capture only what the book states or formalizes.

- `named frameworks or theories`
- `chapter logic`
- `recurring categories or typologies`
- `stated research steps`
- `common verbs of analysis`

## 4. Policy Object Definition

- `what counts as a policy problem`
- `time scale`
- `level of analysis`
- `geography or jurisdiction`
- `unit of comparison`

## 5. Actor Model

- `key actors`
- `who has agency`
- `who is constrained`
- `state/market/society balance`
- `treatment of frontline implementation actors`

## 6. Causal Model

- `main mechanism`
- `causal sequence`
- `where implementation enters`
- `feedback loop or adaptation logic`
- `what most often breaks the chain`

## 7. Evidence Preference

- `cases`
- `comparative history`
- `statistics`
- `institutional documents`
- `interviews`
- `conceptual or normative argument`

Note which evidence type is used most often to settle disagreements.

## 8. Evaluation Logic

- `what counts as success`
- `what counts as failure`
- `explicit metrics`
- `hidden criteria`
- `who gets to judge outcomes`

## 9. Implicit Thinking Model

Fill this section only after repeated signals appear.

- `default starting point`
- `preferred explanatory lens`
- `hidden assumptions`
- `recurring tradeoffs`
- `blind spots`
- `favored recommendation pattern`

## 10. Reusable Heuristics

Convert the book into short operational rules, for example:

- `先定义政策问题，再识别行动者与约束`
- `不要只看目标设计，要看执行链条是否闭合`
- `比较政策时先对齐制度背景`

## 11. Boundaries

- `where the model works well`
- `where it becomes distorted`
- `which topics it under-explains`
- `what kind of transfer requires caution`

## 12. Confidence Statement

Close every record with:

- `confidence`
- `why this confidence level`
- `what additional text would most change the result`
